# Phase diagram

RCWE uses region classification before attempting falsely precise pair-specific parameter labels.

## Regions

- **E — Stable equilibrium:** trajectories decay toward an interior equilibrium.
- **O — Oscillatory:** a limit-cycle regime; supercritical and subcritical onset must be distinguished.
- **M — Multistable / hysteretic:** multiple relationship modes coexist and history or initial condition selects the basin.
- **L — Channel-locked / escape:** the finite interior regime is left and `p_D` tends toward 0 or 1. This is not automatically “breakup” or “couple formation”.
- **B — Bifurcation boundary:** Hopf, saddle-node, Bautin, or another boundary region.

## Main structural results

For `R < 1/4`, a unique finite equilibrium exists when

\[
R<\Delta<1-R.
\]

It is stable below `G_H` and loses stability at the Hopf candidate surface.

For `1/4 < R < 1/2`, multiple equilibria can occur. At symmetry,

\[
t=0,\qquad t=\pm\sqrt{4R-1},
\]

with the central equilibrium acting as a separator. This supports bistability and hysteresis; `G` alone is insufficient to classify the dynamics.

For `R >= 1/2` at symmetry, the central finite equilibrium is a saddle and generic trajectories can escape toward channel locking.

## Interpretive limit

The phase label describes mathematical channel dynamics. It does not, by itself, identify affection, moral value, relationship success, or narrative genre.
