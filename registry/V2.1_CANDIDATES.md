# RCWE v2.1 candidate registry

These ideas are quarantined from v2.0 validation. Inclusion requires repeated evidence, identifiable parameters, and a new freeze.

1. **Origin Channel Persistence** — a salient non-Direct channel present before romantic activation may remain a stable scaffold after romance emerges; requires new subjects selected before their outcomes are known.
2. **Finite-Horizon Effect** — a mutually known deadline may alter approach, inhibition, or event rates; requires a new subject in which the deadline is known before the validation window.
3. **Persona-conditioned channel valuation** — context labels such as a stable persona mode may alter channel values.
4. **State-dependent intervention nodes** — an observer/intervener changes event rate after observing pair states.
5. **Direct inter-pair coupling / Network RCWE** — only after shared events and endogenous mediation fail, with repeated predictive cross-lags.
6. **Constraint action-point taxonomy** — constraints may act on interaction access, transition feasibility, disclosure, or another distinct mechanism.
7. **Separate character-volatility process** — individual emotional motion may be modelled without contaminating dyadic channels.
8. **Static-tension state** — required only if high romantic tension reproducibly occurs with negligible channel AC power.
9. **Explicit marked event process** — a predeclared stochastic model for event rates and marks, fitted without post-hoc signs.
10. **Full four-channel value functions** — a unique, implementable `V_k` family that closes the complete simplex dynamics.

Candidate status is not evidence. Each proposal must name a falsifying observation before promotion.
