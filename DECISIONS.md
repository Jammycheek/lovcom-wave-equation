# Decision record

## Frozen decisions

1. The primary observation is a four-channel simplex: `D/S/C/P`. The former indirect channel is removed.
2. Masking is an attribute, not a channel.
3. Direct intimacy is not relationship approach. Direction is coded separately.
4. No interaction is a gap, not `p_D = 0`.
5. Character belief and analyst posterior are distinct objects.
6. Latent variables (`psi`, `h`, `U`) are inferred, never directly annotated.
7. Validation compares predictions with future blind-coded observations, not inferred latent states with subjective “ground truth”.
8. Interaction time is the default clock for cross-media comparison.
9. The frozen minimal validation target is `p_D`; a unique four-channel value function is not yet specified.
10. External forcing discovered after seeing a subject may not be inserted into the frozen differential equation. Event-triggered jump tests are used instead.
11. Network coupling is a v2.1 candidate and is considered only after shared and endogenous interventions fail.
12. Failures remain visible; they are not rewritten as successes.
