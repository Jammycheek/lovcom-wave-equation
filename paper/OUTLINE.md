# Paper outline

1. **Introduction** — why relationship affection and romantic-comedy tension should be separated; “Love is DC; Rom-Com is AC”.
2. **Construct definition** — dyadic interaction channels, direction, masking, events, character belief, analyst posterior.
3. **RCWE v2.0** — minimal differential system and observation model.
4. **Phase structure** — equilibrium, oscillation, multistability, channel locking, and bifurcation boundaries.
5. **Measurement** — Annotation Codebook v0.2.1, blind coding, provenance, reliability gates.
6. **Tension Bridge** — external love/tension/drama ratings and channel AC power.
7. **Validation designs** — Subject 5 RBV, Subject 6 prospective holdout, Subject 7 blind replication, prospective control.
8. **Results** — report confirmatory and exploratory analyses separately; include negative results.
9. **Failure analysis** — sampling bias, forced versus intrinsic motion, source-resolution bias, and model-identification limits.
10. **Discussion** — narrative oscillator versus psychological oscillator; what RCWE does not claim.
11. **Future work** — v2.1 candidates, only where frozen evidence warrants them.
