2. **Construct definition** — dyadic interaction channels, direction, masking, events, character belief, analyst posterior.
3. **RCWE v2.0** — minimal differential system and observation model.
4. **Phase structure** — equilibrium, oscillation, multistability, channel locking, and bifurcation boundaries.
5. **Measurement** — Annotation Codebook v0.2.1, blind coding, provenance, reliability gates.
6. **Predictive scoring** — open-loop forecast, five-bin observation likelihood, and frozen baselines.
7. **Tension Bridge** — preregistered external love/tension/drama concept and channel AC power; experimental design pending.
8. **Validation designs** — Subject 5 RBV, Subject 6 prospective holdout, Subject 7 blind replication, prospective control.
9. **Results** — report confirmatory and exploratory analyses separately; include negative results.
10. **Failure analysis** — sampling bias, forced versus intrinsic motion, source-resolution bias, and model-identification limits.
11. **Discussion** — narrative oscillator versus psychological oscillator; what RCWE does not claim.
12. **Future work** — v2.1 candidates, only where frozen evidence warrants them.
